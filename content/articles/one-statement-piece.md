Look closely at the best street style photos and most of them follow the same structure: one strong piece, surrounded by calm basics. The quiet pieces give the loud one room to be seen.

## Step 1: choose the hero

It can be a bright jacket, a printed shirt, statement sneakers or a bold bag. Pick one. Two heroes in one outfit usually fight each other.

## Step 2: keep the rest simple

Build around it with neutral colours: black, white, grey, navy, denim. Plain tees, straight trousers, clean shoes. If the hero is on top, keep the bottom half quiet, and the other way around.

![Bright jacket with neutral basics](/images/articles/one-statement-piece/photo-1.svg "A loud jacket over quiet basics.")
![Statement sneakers with a plain outfit](/images/articles/one-statement-piece/photo-2.svg "Let the shoes do the talking.")

## Step 3: repeat one colour

Pull a single colour from the statement piece and repeat it once, small, somewhere else: a sock, a cap, a ring. It ties the outfit together without competing.

That's the whole method. Once it becomes a habit, getting dressed gets faster and your photos get better.
