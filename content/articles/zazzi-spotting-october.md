Every month we walk the same streets with a camera and look for the people who make us stop. October gave us a lot to work with. The weather finally cooled down, jackets came back out, and people started layering again.

The biggest pattern was denim worn big. Wide jeans with a long break at the ankle, denim jackets two sizes up, and more than one full denim-on-denim look that worked because the washes didn't match.

![Wide jeans and a cropped jacket](/images/articles/zazzi-spotting-october/photo-1.svg "Wide denim, cropped top layer.")
![Denim on denim in two washes](/images/articles/zazzi-spotting-october/photo-2.svg "Two washes, one outfit.")

## Socks are the new statement

Shorter trousers mean socks are on show, and people are using them. Red, striped, logo-heavy, or deliberately mismatched. It's the cheapest way to make a plain outfit look intentional.

## The jacket that did everything

Our favourite look of the month was the simplest: a worn leather jacket over a hoodie, straight jeans and old sneakers. Nothing new, nothing loud, and it still stopped us in the middle of the street.

> The best outfits we see are rarely the most expensive. They're the ones that look like they belong to the person wearing them.
