Big, rounded legs. A sharp taper at the ankle. A silhouette that's hard to miss. **Nikka-pokka trousers**, also known as **Tobi pants**, have long been part of Japan's construction workwear wardrobe. These days, their oversized proportions are turning up in fashion collections and streetwear looks, giving everyday outfits a distinctly Japanese edge.



![A metallic silver tracksuit featuring oversized, rounded trousers with a silhouette inspired by traditional Nikka-pokka pants](/images/articles/japanese-workwear-from-traditional-uniforms-to-modern-street-fashion/workwear-article2-4.jpg "A metallic silver tracksuit featuring oversized, rounded trousers with a silhouette inspired by traditional Nikka-pokka pants  | Photo by Netta Meisles")




## The Shape of Japanese Workwear

Worn by *tobi* workers, the specialists who build and work on scaffolding, Nikka-pokka trousers are designed with movement in mind. The extra room through the thighs allows workers to climb, bend and move freely, while the narrow cuffs keep the fabric away from their feet. The result is a striking shape: almost balloon-like through the leg, then pulled in dramatically at the ankle.

That silhouette feels right at home in today's oversized fashion landscape. Styled with a cropped jacket, a fitted top or a pair of chunky shoes, Nikka-pokka trousers bring volume and character without much effort. Their proportions also sit naturally alongside other Japanese fashion staples, including traditional *tabi* footwear.

## Where to Shop: TORAICHI KANDA




For those looking to bring Japanese workwear into their own wardrobe, **TORAICHI KANDA** in Tokyo's Kanda district is well worth a stop.

![The exterior storefront of TORAICHI KANDA in Tokyo](/images/articles/japanese-workwear-from-traditional-uniforms-to-modern-street-fashion/workwear-article8-2.jpg "TORAICHI KANDA, bringing a fresh fashion perspective to Japanese workwear | Photo by Netta Meisles")



This local design brand gives classic workwear cuts a chic, contemporary twist, using a diverse range of modern textiles to create stylish pieces for everyday wear. Its cool shop is packed with high-quality pieces that give classic workwear a modern and stylish reinterpretation.
