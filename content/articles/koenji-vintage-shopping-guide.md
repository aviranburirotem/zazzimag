Koenji is full of surprises. The neighbourhood is home to independent vintage stores run by people with an exceptional eye for clothing. Here, vintage is more than secondhand shopping. It's about finding original pieces, recognising their history and knowing what makes them special.

We picked four shops worth seeking out. Consider this your introduction to a different side of Tokyo's vintage scene.

### 01. SALERS



- **Instagram:** [@salerstokyo](https://www.instagram.com/salerstokyo/)
- **Address:** 4-22-2 Koenjiminami, Suginami-ku, Tokyo



A little Americana, a little Western, and a lot of character. SALERS specialises in secondhand clothing sourced directly from the US, with a selection ranging from denim and workwear to Western boots, suede shirts and classic American labels. And there's a lovely bonus: Okome, the shop's resident poodle, is often there to welcome visitors.

### 02. 7DAYS



- **Instagram:** [@7days_koenji](https://www.instagram.com/7days_koenji/)  
- **Online store:** [7DAYS BASE](https://furugiya7day.base.shop/)  
- **Address:** 3-49-3 Koenjiminami, Kitaichi Building A, 1F, Suginami-ku, Tokyo



7DAYS has an unmistakably eclectic atmosphere, with quirky furniture and accessories inspired by the 1960s through the 1990s. Its carefully arranged interiors, vintage objects and clothing give the shop a distinctive look, with plenty of interesting details to discover as you browse.


![funky2](/images/articles/koenji-vintage-shopping-guide/funky2.jpg "")


### 03. sleeping yet



- **Instagram:** [@sleeping_yet_](https://www.instagram.com/sleeping_yet_/)  
- **Online store:** [sleeping yet BASE](https://sleepingyet.base.shop/)  
- **Address:** New Koenji Mansion 1-B, 4-23-2 Koenjiminami, Suginami-ku, Tokyo



Small, independent and full of personality. sleeping yet brings together carefully selected vintage clothing, original creations and embroidered pieces. The shop has a playful identity and a distinct approach to its selection, making it a refreshing stop for anyone who enjoys discovering something a little different.

![funky21 2](/images/articles/koenji-vintage-shopping-guide/funky21-2.jpg "")



### 04. 70s vintage shop hiragi



- **Instagram:** [@70s_vintage_shop_hiragi](https://www.instagram.com/70s_vintage_shop_hiragi/)  
- **Address:** 3-45-9 Koenjiminami, Gracia M, C, Suginami-ku, Tokyo



70s vintage shop hiragi specialises in authentic vintage clothing from the late 1960s and 1970s. Its selection includes an impressive range of flared denim and bell-bottoms, alongside hippie-inspired custom designs. A great destination for anyone interested in the distinctive silhouettes and aesthetics of the era.

![funky21](/images/articles/koenji-vintage-shopping-guide/funky21.jpg "")



### There's Always More to Find

These four shops are just a glimpse of what Koenji has to offer. The neighbourhood is full of independent stores, each with its own taste, character and approach to vintage clothing.

Give yourself time to wander, explore the side streets and follow your curiosity. Step into the small shops you come across along the way and take a closer look at their collections. You never know what you'll find.
