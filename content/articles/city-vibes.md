Every city has neighbourhoods with their own dress code, even if nobody wrote it down. The area around the market dresses differently from the office district, and both look nothing like the streets near the beach.

## The market crowd

Comfortable, practical and full of second-hand finds. Loose trousers, tote bags, sandals for as long as the weather allows. Style here is about ease.

![Market street style](/images/articles/city-vibes/photo-1.svg "Easy layers near the market.")
![Evening street style](/images/articles/city-vibes/photo-2.svg "After dark, the jackets come out.")

## After dark

At night the same streets get sharper. More black, more leather, more jewellery. Outfits are built to go from dinner to a bar without going home first.

## Why it matters

The places we spend time in shape what we wear, often without us noticing. Paying attention to that is one of the best ways to understand a city, and one of the reasons we keep going out with a camera.
