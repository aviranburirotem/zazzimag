Trend cycles move so fast now that keeping up has stopped being the point. What we see on the street instead is people building a look that's recognisably theirs and sticking with it.

That might be a colour someone wears every day, a vintage piece they rework into every outfit, or a way of styling a cap that becomes a signature. The common thread is confidence.

## Mixing high and low

The most interesting outfits mix things that aren't supposed to go together: a thrifted blazer with technical trainers, a football shirt under a tailored coat, gold jewellery with a tracksuit. The mix is the message.

## How to start

- Pick one thing you love and wear it more often than feels normal.
- Buy less, but buy pieces that only you would choose.
- Pay attention to what people compliment. That's usually your style talking.

Self-expression isn't a trend that will fade next season. It's what's left when you stop following them.
