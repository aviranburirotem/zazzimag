Second-hand shopping is a great way to find interesting clothes without stretching your budget. Tokyo offers everything from affordable everyday finds to vintage pieces and designer labels. Knowing the difference between its shops and resale models can help you find exactly what you're looking for.

## Thrift Stores, Resale Shops & Consignment: What's the Difference?

**Thrift stores** sell a broad mix of pre-owned clothing, shoes and accessories, often at accessible prices. In Japan, these are more commonly called reuse shops or second-hand shops.

**Resale shops** buy items directly from individuals and resell them. This is the most common model among Japanese chains such as 2nd STREET, KINDAL, BAZZSTORE and MODE OFF.

**Consignment stores** sell items on behalf of their owners, who receive payment only after a sale, minus the store's commission. This model is more common among specialist boutiques. In Tokyo, GINZA CELIA and REFIND Tokyo offer consignment services.

## A Changing Second-Hand Market

Japan's second-hand market has a history closely tied to the country's changing economy. After the bubble economy collapsed in the early 1990s, Japan entered decades of economic stagnation and deflation. As consumers became more price-conscious, reuse shops grew into an established part of everyday retail, offering an alternative to buying new.

Today, the market is changing again. Japanese second-hand fashion has attracted growing international attention, fuelled by social media, tourism, online resale and a weak yen. Overseas shoppers and resellers are increasingly competing for sought-after vintage and designer pieces, pushing prices up in some parts of the market.

## A Note on Authenticity

Japan prohibits the import of counterfeit goods, but not every second-hand item is automatically guaranteed authentic. Specialist retailers such as RAGTAG and REFIND Tokyo have their own authentication procedures. When buying luxury fashion, check the seller's reputation and authentication policy.

## Where to Shop: Second-Hand Stores & Flea Markets

Tokyo has plenty of options, whether you're looking for designer labels, everyday bargains or the thrill of finding something unexpected.

**Second-hand shops:**

- **TreFacStyle:** Contemporary brands, designer clothing, shoes and accessories.
- **KINDAL:** Japanese and international designer labels, streetwear and luxury fashion.
- **BAZZSTORE:** Branded and unbranded second-hand clothing.
- **MODE OFF:** Everyday clothing, bags, shoes and accessories, often at accessible prices.
- **2nd STREET:** A wide selection, from casual wear to designer labels.
- **RAGTAG:** A second-hand fashion retailer specialising in Japanese and international designer labels.
- **Caliente Vintage Culture:** American vintage clothing, accessories and lifestyle items in Kichijōji.

Designer-focused shops such as KINDAL and RAGTAG can be more expensive, while MODE OFF and some BAZZSTORE locations are worth exploring for lower-priced finds.


![2c4a5610](/images/articles/tokyo-second-hand-shopping/2c4a5610-2.jpg "")


**Flea markets:**

- **Tokyo City Flea Market:** At Oi Racecourse, with hundreds of vendors selling clothing, accessories and household goods on selected weekends.
- **MOTTAINAI Flea Market:** A travelling market held at different Tokyo venues, including occasional events at Shinjuku Central Park.
- **Shimokitazawa Flea Market:** An outdoor market at Shimokita Senrogai Akichi, featuring vintage clothing, accessories and handmade items.

Whether browsing designer resale shops or searching through flea-market stalls, Tokyo offers plenty of ways to explore second-hand fashion. Prices may have changed, but there are still interesting pieces to be found at different budgets. Sometimes, the best finds are simply a matter of knowing where to look and taking the time to explore.
